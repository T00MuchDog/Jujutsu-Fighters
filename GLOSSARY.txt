================================================================================
  0. NEW PLAYER TUTORIAL
================================================================================

START A LOCAL FIGHT
  1. From the main menu choose SINGLE PLAYER.
  2. Choose 1V1 for the simplest first match, or 2V2 for a team fight.
  3. Leave STAT MODE: STANDARD for normal character stats, or toggle to
     STAT MODE: EQUALIZED to blend runtime stats halfway toward the baseline of
     80 without changing moves or abilities.
  4. Pick your fighter(s), then the CPU fighter(s). Choose a MOVE SET when the
     selected fighter has more than one; a fighter with no selected set cannot be
     confirmed. RANDOM FIGHTER chooses an unpicked fighter and, when necessary,
     builds a legal battle-only random move set.
  5. On the battle screen choose START BATTLE.

PLAN A ROUND
  Each fighter has an OFFENSE timeline and a DEFENSE timeline. They are separate
  boards, so offensive and defensive segments may occupy the same ticks, but both
  boards share that fighter's AP and CE budgets.

  • Click a move card to place it in the first legal gap, or drag it onto a
    timeline to choose its start tick.
  • Drag a placed segment to retime it. Right-click it, or drag it off the
    timeline, to remove it. Gaps are allowed; segments on the same board cannot
    overlap or extend past the grid.
  • In team fights, targeted moves ask for an ENEMY, ALLY, or set of TARGETS.
    Click a placed targeted segment to change its target before locking in.
  • Choose LOCK IN (shown as LOCK in the compact layout) when satisfied. In 2V2,
    use the page arrows or LEFT/RIGHT to plan each fighter; the team plan submits
    only after both fighters are locked.

READ THE TIMELINE
  A move occupies its AP Cost in ticks. Its important timing is its fire tick:

      fireTick = startTick + unleashPoint - 1

  Example: a move placed at tick 3 with Unleash 2 fires at tick 4. CE is shown as
  reserved while planning but is actually paid when the segment starts. If prior
  actions, upkeep, or drains leave too little CE at that moment, the move is
  cancelled while its AP remains spent.

  When different fighters act on the same tick, instant moves (Unleash 1) have
  priority, then higher Speed, then a random tie-break. If one fighter has an
  offensive and defensive move firing together, that fighter's defense fires
  first. Multi-hit moves may land later than the parent segment's fire tick; each
  hit resolves on its own impact tick.

DEFEND
  • BLOCK reduces covered damage. GUARD_BREAK can bypass it and a stronger attack
    can beat a lower-Potency block.
  • PARRY negates a covered hit and may stagger a melee attacker. INTANGIBLE
    attacks bypass parries.
  • DODGE rolls its listed chance against the ranges it covers. A successful roll
    avoids the hit completely.
  • A FIXED defense opens on its fire tick. Fire it before the expected impact;
    merely sharing a tick is not enough if the faster attack resolves first.
  • A REACTION defense arms on its fire tick and opens when a matching attack
    arrives. It is easier to time but cannot earn a Perfect Read.
  • Read every defense card for its Potency, attack/range/element coverage,
    duration, use limit, and timing. These properties are move-specific.

RESOLVE AND WIN
  After all plans are locked, both sides resolve tick by tick. Review the round,
  choose NEXT ROUND, and plan again. The fight ends when one side has no living
  fighters; living summons do not keep a defeated team in the fight. Elimination
  within one deferred multi-target/recoil resolution batch can produce a draw;
  otherwise battle-over is checked after each firing move.

BEGINNER TACTICS
  Start with one affordable attack and one defense instead of spending every AP
  and CE point. Compare fire ticks rather than only segment positions, place fixed
  defenses slightly before the impact you expect, and preserve CE for later
  rounds and upkeep. In 2V2, verify both fighters' targets before LOCK IN and
  focus attacks when removing one enemy is more valuable than splitting damage.

================================================================================
  1. CHARACTER STATS  (StatKey.java, CharacterStats.java)
================================================================================

The ten raw base stats defined per character. StatKey is the single source of
truth: each constant owns its camelCase JSON field name, display label, and
lookup aliases.

  Range:      MIN_STAT = 10 (minimum) to MAX_STAT = 300 (maximum).
  Baseline:   BASELINE = 80 (default, and the point-buy starting value).
  N/A:        value 0 means the stat is absent. Used for CURSED_TECHNIQUE_MASTERY
              on characters without an innate technique. Editors are unclamped;
              gameplay clamps to [10, 300].

  StatKey constants (label / aliases):

    VITALITY                       vitality / vit
      Determines maximum HP.
      HP = round(S(Vitality) × HP_PER_VIT),  HP_PER_VIT = 3.5  (S = StatScale).
      Baseline 80 → 280 HP. Max 300 → ~1652 HP (S(300)=472 — see STAT SCALING).

    STRENGTH                       strength / str
      Physical striking power. Primary driver of Power for PHYSICAL moves
      (4:1 with Combat Ability).

    DURABILITY                     durability / dur
      Physical resilience. Weighted 2 in the Defense formula (alongside CE
      reinforcement, weighted 6).

    SPEED                          speed / spd
      Movement and reaction speed. Primary driver of AP Bar size and Evasion;
      secondary driver of Accuracy.

    COMBAT_ABILITY                 combatAbility / ca
      General combat aptitude. Primary driver of Accuracy; secondary driver of
      AP Bar size and PHYSICAL Power. Determines the COMBAT_ARTS slot budget.

    CURSED_ENERGY_RESERVES         cursedEnergyReserves / cer
      Total CE pool available in a fight. Weighted 2 in the CE Power component
      and supplies the CE term of Defense.

    CURSED_ENERGY_EFFICIENCY       cursedEnergyEfficiency / ceeff
      Reduces the CE cost of CE-tagged moves (scaled stat). 80 = 1.0× cost.
      See "CE COST SCALING" for the full Efficiency × Output formula.

    CURSED_ENERGY_OUTPUT           cursedEnergyOutput / ceo
      Raw cursed energy output. Primary driver (4:2:1) of the CE Power component,
      caps CE reinforcement in the Defense formula (× DEFENSE_CE_CAP_FACTOR), and
      raises the CE cost of CE-tagged moves above the baseline (scaled: ≤80 = 1.0×, 472 = 3.0×).

    JUJUTSU_SKILL                  jujutsuSkill / js
      Governs NON_INNATE_TECHNIQUE moves and barriers / RCT / cursed-tool styles.
      Determines the JUJUTSU_ARTS slot budget. Secondary AP Bar driver.

    CURSED_TECHNIQUE_MASTERY       cursedTechniqueMastery / ctm
      Only meaningful for characters with an innate cursed technique. Scales
      INNATE_TECHNIQUE Power (50:50 with the CE component).
      Does NOT grant move slots — slots come from JUJUTSU_SKILL and COMBAT_ABILITY
      only. Set to 0 (N/A) for characters without an innate technique.

  Lookup: StatKey.fromString normalises to lowercase and strips spaces and
  underscores before matching aliases or fieldName. Throws on no match.

BASE STAT TOTAL (BST)
  The sum of all ten raw authored CharacterStats values before StatScale or
  ability modifiers. Cursed Spirit Physiology uses this raw total to determine
  which low authored CE costs are waived.

  Character Select displays construction-time passive-adjusted stats, including
  passive-adjusted Max HP and Max CE. Its on-screen "Base Stat Total" is therefore
  a display total and may differ from canonical raw BST; combat rules that name
  BST always mean the raw authored total above. If a preview combatant cannot be
  constructed, Character Select falls back to the authored values.

================================================================================
  3. COMBAT STATS  (CombatStats.java)
================================================================================

Derived values computed from base stats at combatant load time, plus the static
damage-resolution helpers. These are the numbers actually used in combat.

  Tuning constants (lines noted):
    HP_PER_VIT            = 3.5      (L99,  final)
    AP_DIVISOR            = 18       (L108, final)
    HIT_BALANCE_FACTOR    = 1/19     (L115, final)  → equal ACC/EVA = 95% on a 1.0 move
    CE_POOL_SCALE         = 8        (L118, final)
    ART_SLOT_TIERS        = table    (see §7)  → baseline 80 = 6 slots, max 15 at 300
    BF_BASE_CHANCE        = 0.03     (L124, PLACEHOLDER)
    BFS_BF_CHANCES        = {0.10, 0.20, 0.35, 0.50}  (L127, final)
    BF_DAMAGE_MULTIPLIER  = 2.5      (L130, final)
    BF_CE_RESTORE_FRACTION= 0.05     (L133, PLACEHOLDER)
    DEFENSE_CE_CAP_FACTOR = 0.5      (L142, final)
    PHYSICAL_POWER_MULT   = 0.85     (after CombatStats constants; PHYSICAL only)

  STAT SCALING (StatScale.java) — applied to EVERY base stat before any formula:
    S(s) = max(8, round(80 + sign(s − 80) · 0.12 · |s − 80|^1.5)),  anchored at S(80)=80.
    Flattens at the baseline (derivative 0) and steepens away: stat differences matter
    MORE the further from 80 — a 300-vs-250 gap dwarfs a 100-vs-200 gap — while low
    stats stay meaningfully worse without collapsing (S(20)=24, S(40)=50, S(60)=69).
    Applied symmetrically to both sides of every comparison (Power/Defense, ACC/EVA…).
    Sample: s= 10 20 40 60 80 100 120 150 200 250 300
            S= 10 24 50 69 80  91 110 150 238 346 472

  Stored derived values (computed once per combatant; inputs are SCALED via S()):
    Max HP          = round(S(Vitality) × HP_PER_VIT)
    Max AP Bar      = (S(Speed) × 15 + S(CombatAbility) × 3) / AP_DIVISOR  → baseline 80
    Accuracy        = (S(CombatAbility) × 4 + S(Speed)) / 5                 → baseline 80
    Evasion         = (S(Speed) × 4 + S(CombatAbility)) / 5                → baseline 80
    Max CE (pool)   = round(S(CursedEnergyReserves) × CE_POOL_SCALE)       → baseline 80 = 640
    Combat Arts slots = artSlotsFor(CombatAbility)   ← RAW stat, threshold table (§7) → baseline 6
    Jujutsu Arts slots= artSlotsFor(JujutsuSkill)    ← RAW stat, threshold table (§7) → baseline 6

  DEFENSE — computed per hit, NOT stored.
    The defender's resilience at the moment of each hit. CE reinforcement is
    CAPPED by Cursed Energy Output, NOT by pool size. While the pool can supply
    at least the cap, Defense holds at a plateau — spending CE early in a round
    costs no Defense. Once the pool drops below the cap, Defense degrades
    (steeply, since the CE term is weighted 6). Lore-accurate: a bottomless-CE
    character (e.g. Yuta, Hakari) still has finite Defense because their OUTPUT,
    not their pool, limits reinforcement.

      ceFromPool       = S(CursedEnergyReserves) × (currentCe / maxCe)
      reinforcementCap = S(CursedEnergyOutput) × DEFENSE_CE_CAP_FACTOR     (= × 0.5)
      ceReinforcement  = min(ceFromPool, reinforcementCap)
      Defense          = round((ceReinforcement × 6 + S(Durability) × 2) / 6)

    Pipeline: incoming damage → DEFENSIVE move reduction (block) → Defense → final.
    Key clarifications:
      - Defense IS a combat stat (computed, not a stored base stat). "Defense
        stat" is acceptable shorthand for the computed value.
      - CURSED_ENERGY_OUTPUT_INCREASE raises the reinforcement cap.
      - MODIFY_DEFENSE multiplies the effective Defense value.
      - Do NOT confuse with DEFENSIVE (a MoveTag for blocking moves).
      - Do NOT confuse blockDamageReduction (block %) with Defense.

  HIT CHANCE (static):
      hitChance = min(1.0, baseAccuracy × attackerAccuracy
                              / (attackerAccuracy + defenderEvasion × HIT_BALANCE_FACTOR))
    Equal ACC/EVA on a 1.0-base-accuracy move → 95%.

================================================================================
  4. MOVE STRUCTURE  (Move.java, MoveData.java)
================================================================================

Move is the immutable domain object; MoveData is its JSON DTO. Field names below
are the Move/MoveData field names.

  Identity / classification
    id                    6-digit zero-padded string (e.g. "000003"). Auto-assigned
                          by MoveRepository; resequenced on delete.
    name, description     Display text.
    moveTypes             Authored learning classes for NON-TECHNIQUE moves. Such
                          a move must retain at least one class; CURSED_SPIRIT moves
                          are class-restricted and must explicitly carry
                          CURSED_ENERGY. A move with requiredTechniqueId is a
                          TECHNIQUE MOVE: its class list is ignored and omitted on
                          save because any character type possessing or unlocking
                          that technique may learn it.
    category              MoveCategory (derived from tags; see §6).
    tags                  Set<MoveTag> — the original data tags, retained for
                          UI, filtering, ability scoping, and round-tripping.
    pool                  MovePool (Combat Arts vs Jujutsu Arts; see §7).

  Combat numbers
    basePower             Combined raw damage number across every hit component.
                          0 for non-attacking moves.
    hitComponents         Ordered damage instances. Each stores basePower, its own
                          HIT TAGS, a delayTicks offset from unleash,
                          requiresPreviousConnection, avoidable, and optional
                          accuracy. Hit tags may include damage type, MELEE/RANGED,
                          GUARD_BREAK, INTANGIBLE, and elemental tags. Power,
                          defense coverage, reactions, and Black Flash are resolved
                          from the current component, so different hits in one move
                          can behave differently. Legacy attacks without this list
                          synthesize one component from the move-level fields.
    baseAccuracy          Fraction [0.0, 1.0] — inherent accuracy before ACC/EVA.
                          1.0 = 100% (still subject to the hit roll).
    neverMiss             If true, the move bypasses the hit roll and always hits.
    apCost                 AP ticks the move occupies on the AP Timeline. The
                          segment spans [startTick, startTick + apCost - 1].
    unleashPoint          Tick within the segment at which the move fires.
                          Range [1, apCost]. 1 = instant (highest priority);
                          == apCost = full charge (fires last).

  Cursed Energy cost
    baseCeCost            CE cost before Efficiency/Output scaling.
    hasCeCost             Whether the move has a CE cost. False = no CE cost and
                          CE is omitted from the card. True = any cost from 0 up.
    minCeCost             Floor: cost scaling cannot reduce cost below this.
    maxCeCost             Ceiling: cost scaling cannot raise cost above this.

  Defensive data (used only when defenseType != NONE; see §8)
    defenseType           NONE / BLOCK / PARRY / DODGE / SHIELD.
    blockStyle            PERCENTAGE / FLAT. Used only when defenseType = BLOCK.
    defenseTiming         FIXED opens at the defense's fire tick. REACTION arms at
                          the fire tick and opens when the next matching hostile
                          attack arrives. Shared by BLOCK, PARRY, and DODGE.
    blockDuration         0 = one whole AP segment's duration; -1 = end of round;
                          >0 = that many ticks after the window opens. For FIXED the
                          anchor is the fire tick; for REACTION it is the trigger
                          tick.
    defenseUses           Number of attacks the active defense may contest. 0 =
                          unlimited. The use cap does not shorten the time window.
    blockAttackTypes      Accepted defensive attack categories: PHYSICAL,
                          PHYSICAL_CURSED_ENERGY, CURSED_ENERGY. Empty = all.
    blockRanges           Accepted MELEE/RANGED hit tags. Empty = all, including
                          untagged hits.
    blockElementalTags    Accepted ICE/ELECTRIC/FIRE/WATER hit tags. Empty = all.
                          These three per-hit profiles apply to BLOCK and PARRY;
                          blockAffectedTags is a legacy migration field only.
    blockDamageReduction  0–100, PERCENTAGE block only; conditional effectiveness
                          determines whether the live reduction reaches 100.
    blockFlatReduction    FLAT block only. Subtracted from attack value before
                          ordinary Defense, with an attack-value floor of 1.
    dodgeChance           0–100%. DODGE only — probability to avoid a matching
                          incoming attack entirely (no damage). Chance-based;
                          not potency-gated.
    dodgeScope            MELEE / RANGED / BOTH. DODGE only — which attack ranges
                          the dodge reacts to.
    parryStaggerTicks     AP ticks to STAGGER the attacker on a successful parry
                          of a non-GUARD_BREAK attack. PARRY only. 0 = no stagger.
    potency               1–5. Gates defence vs attack: a BLOCK/PARRY applies iff
                          its potency ≥ the attack's potency. Always 1 for utility.

  Effects (canonical: unified effects array)
    effects               List<MoveEffectData> — the authoritative composition of a
                          move's non-damage behaviour. Each row reuses the shared
                          AbilityEffectType vocabulary (§14) plus move-only
                          activation context. When non-null it is authoritative over
                          the legacy attachment fields; MoveData.fromMove emits only
                          this list for unified moves (Move.usesUnifiedEffects).
    trigger               MoveEffectTrigger — when the row fires:
                            AVAILABILITY    gates whether the move can be planned
                                            (e.g. MOVE_UNAVAILABLE_WHILE_OWNED_SUMMON_ACTIVE).
                            ACCURACY_CHECK  modifies the hit roll (NEVER_MISS / NEVER_HIT).
                            ON_START        when the segment begins, before CE is
                                            paid; consumption remains spent if a
                                            later interruption cancels the move.
                            ON_FIRE         on unleash (hit/miss/block agnostic).
                            ON_HIT          on a connected hit (may bind to one
                                            hitComponent via hitComponentIndex).
                            BLOCK_CALCULATION  while this BLOCK calculates its
                                            reduction against the current hit.
                            ON_BLOCK / ON_PARRY / ON_DODGE  on the matching defense.
    Per-row shape         type (AbilityEffectType) + target (SELF / ENEMY / BOTH) +
                          an optional condition + duration/magnitude as the type
                          requires + coded fields (codedAbilityKey / codedAction /
                          codedTarget / codedStackCount / codedParameters) + an
                          optional masteryProgression map (modes FORMULA / BENCHMARKS,
                          resolved by TechniqueMasteryResolver; progression is gated
                          to INNATE_TECHNIQUE-tagged moves).
    Status / Coded / Summon   The three canonical row kinds map to APPLY_STATUS
                          (a StatusEffectType + duration), CODED_MOVE_ACTION
                          (codedAbilityKey + codedAction; dispatched to a
                          CodedAbilityRuntime, §15), and SUMMON_CHARACTER
                          (characterId = a SHIKIGAMI definition id; enqueues a
                          shikigami that materializes after the current tick batch
                          and joins planning next round. Only SHIKIGAMI definitions
                          may be summoned).
    Legacy attachment     onHitEffects / selfEffects / onBlockEffects /
                          onParryEffects / onDodgeEffects — the pre-unified
                          StatusEffectData lists (the old STATUS / CODED / SUMMON
                          flavours). Retained for backward compatibility and migrated
                          into the unified list by MoveData.migrateLegacyEffects().

    Summon stat scaling (SummonStatScaler.java) — at materialization each of the
    shikigami's RAW base stats is multiplied by a factor derived from the SUMMONER,
    then clamped to [10, 300] (CTM allows 0). The shikigami's own abilities then
    apply on top of the scaled raw stats as usual.
      governing = round((2·primary + Output) / 3)          2:1 weighting, primary below
      primary   = CTM  if the summoning move is INNATE_TECHNIQUE-tagged (e.g. Ten Shadows)
                = JujutsuSkill otherwise (non-innate summon)
      sg        = S(governing)                             the StatScale curve above
      factor    = 0.5 + (sg − 10)·0.5/70          for sg ≤ 80      (10→0.5x, 80→1.0x)
                = 1.0 + (sg − 80)·1.0/392         for sg >  80      (80→1.0x, 472→2.0x)
                clamped to [0.5, 2.0]             (472 = S(300), the max governing stat)
      → each raw stat becomes clamp(round(raw·factor), 10, 300).
      Neutral point: a baseline summoner (primary = Output = 80) gives factor 1.0 and
      reproduces the authored stats exactly. A peak summoner (300/300) doubles them.

  Area of effect (MoveTag.AOE + AoeType)
    aoeType               Authoritative targeting shape for an AOE-tagged ATTACK
                          or hostile UTILITY move
                          (AoeType.java): MULTIPLE / ALL_ENEMIES / ALL_OTHERS. Defaults
                          to ALL_ENEMIES when blank; FRIENDLY_FIRE tag migrates to
                          ALL_OTHERS. Drives MoveTargeting (NONE / SINGLE_ENEMY /
                          MULTIPLE_ENEMIES / ALL_ENEMIES / ALL_OTHERS). Targets are
                          snapshotted at fire time; later summons are excluded.
    aoeTargetCount        MULTIPLE only — maximum selected enemies hit (min 2).
                          Planning requires 1..N distinct active enemies. Invalid
                          targets are refilled in stable roster order at fire time,
                          but never beyond the number originally selected.

  Ordered pair targeting (Targeting)
    targeting             DEFAULT / SELF_AND_ENEMY / SELF_AND_ALLY /
                          ALLY_AND_ENEMY. SELF is implicit; selected target ids
                          contain only the named non-self endpoints, in enum order.
                          Legacy pairTargeting JSON is accepted as an alias.

  Learning / gating
    prerequisites         Map<stat name → minimum value>. All must be met to
                          learn and use the move.
    requiredTechniqueId   Innate technique name the character must possess.
                          Null/blank = no restriction. (Innate-tech-tagged moves
                          must set this and a cursedTechniqueMastery prerequisite;
                          non-innate-tech-tagged moves must declare a jujutsuSkill
                          prerequisite — enforced by the Move builder.)
    isFreeMove            If true, the move does not consume a move slot. Still
                          respects prerequisites, technique restrictions, and CE/AP.
    mustBeGranted         If true, the move cannot be assigned directly in the
                          Character Editor; it must be made available through a
                          GRANT_MOVE or UNLOCK_MOVE ability effect.
    (weapon tag)          A move carrying one of the weapon-type tags (KATANA,
                          BOW, GREAT_AXE, POLEARM, STAFF) can only be learned by
                          a character with that weapon type equipped (base weapon
                          or cursed tool). Authored via the Move Editor's Weapon
                          Type dropdown.

  Legacy / modifier flags
    guardBreak  Legacy move-wide field migrated onto each hit component.
    heavy       Backs the move-level HEAVY tag and protects the whole segment from
                STUN_CURRENT_ACTION.

  Other accessors worth knowing:
    isBlackFlashEligible()  true when any individual hit component is eligible.
    isMelee() / isRanged() / isGuardBreak() / isIntangible()
                            aggregate "any hit" queries. They are conveniences for
                            filtering; the resolver still uses the current hit.
    isDefensive()           derived from category == DEFENSIVE.
    isBlock() / isParry() / isDodge()  derived from defenseType.
    isActiveDefense()       true for BLOCK/PARRY/DODGE (has a tracked window).
    dodgeAppliesTo(move)   DODGE scope vs an incoming attack's range.
    parryStaggersAttacker(move, hit)  true only for a covered, non-GUARD_BREAK,
                                      non-RANGED hit when stagger ticks are positive.
    blocksAttack(move, hit)  checks the current hit against all three defense
                              coverage dimensions.
    applyBlockTo(value, multiplier)  applies authored block reduction after
                                      conditional effectiveness modifiers.

================================================================================
  5. MOVE TAGS & HIT TAGS  (MoveTag.java, HitComponent.java)
================================================================================

MOVE TAGS describe the parent action: ATTACK / UTILITY / DEFENSIVE, AOE,
FRIENDLY_FIRE, HEAVY, weapon requirements, and learning/filtering metadata.

HIT TAGS describe one damage instance: damage type, range, GUARD_BREAK,
INTANGIBLE, and elements. A HitComponent validates and derives its own category.
Power, Black Flash, block/parry coverage, and elemental reactions use that current
hit. Move.hasTag and Move.isMelee/isRanged/isGuardBreak/isIntangible aggregate
hit-only tags for filtering convenience; that does not make them parent-owned.
Parent type tags are normally synchronized from the union of component type tags.

  TYPE HIT TAGS (select the hit's Power formula / MoveCategory):
    PHYSICAL              Pure physical strike (fists, weapons, body).
                          Governed by Strength + Combat Ability.
    CURSED_ENERGY         Raw cursed energy without a specific technique. May
                          stand alone as a dedicated CURSED_ENERGY category, or
                          combine with PHYSICAL / technique tags.
                          Governed by CE Output, CE Reserves, CE Efficiency.
    INNATE_TECHNIQUE      Move tied to a specific innate cursed technique
                          (e.g. Miracles, Ratio). Requires requiredTechniqueId.
                          Governed by the CE component + Cursed Technique Mastery.
    NON_INNATE_TECHNIQUE  Move from a learned, non-innate technique (barriers,
                          RCT, cursed-tool styles such as New Shadow Style).
                          Governed by the CE component + Jujutsu Skill.

  FUNCTION TAGS:
    ATTACK                Marks an offensive action. Applied alongside a damage
                          type tag. Non-ATTACK moves prompt for no power/accuracy
                          in the editor.
    UTILITY               Non-damaging move (buffs, marks, resource moves). Not
                          slot-gated by category; counted under Jujutsu Arts
                          unless it carries PHYSICAL.
    DEFENSIVE             A move that blocks, parries, dodges, or otherwise
                          protects against incoming damage. BLOCK applies BEFORE Defense
                          in the damage pipeline.

  RANGE HIT TAGS (MoveTag.RANGE_TAGS):
    MELEE                 Close-quarters strike. Modifier tag: does not affect
                          Power, category, or Black Flash eligibility. Lives only
                          in the tag set (no dedicated Move flag).
    RANGED                Distance strike (projectile, beam, thrown weapon).
                          Same rules as MELEE.

  AOE / FRIENDLY-FIRE MOVE TAGS:
    AOE                  Marks an action that resolves against multiple
                         combatants. It supports attacks and hostile utility
                         effects. Paired with aoeType and aoeTargetCount (§4).
    FRIENDLY_FIRE        On an AOE move, migrates aoeType to ALL_OTHERS so the
                         attack can also hit allies. Lives only in the tag set.

  WEAPON TAGS (a move carries at most one; each pairs with a WeaponType):
    KATANA                Katana / katana-like blade.
    BOW                   Bow.
    GREAT_AXE             Great axe.
    POLEARM               Spear / polearm / three-section pole.
    STAFF                 Staff.
    A weapon-tagged move requires the matching weapon equipped (CharacterData
    equippedWeaponTypes, or a cursed tool in equippedCursedToolIds). A cursed
    tool of that type also zeroes the move's CE cost.

  COMBAT MODIFIER HIT TAG:
    INTANGIBLE            A hit carrying this tag cannot be parried and ignores
                          all BLOCK reduction, including full blocks. Dodge,
                          accuracy, ordinary Defense, coded resistance, and damage
                          immunity are unaffected. Lives only in the tag set.

  ELEMENTAL HIT TAGS (MoveTag.ELEMENTAL_TAGS; authored per hit component):
    ICE                   A connected hit has a 5% chance to apply FROZEN, raised
                          to 50% when the target is WET. An ICE hit always cures
                          BURNED.
    ELECTRIC              A connected hit has a 10% chance to stun the target's
                          current action. Damage is doubled against WET targets.
    FIRE                  A connected hit has a 10% chance to apply BURNED for one
                          round and always cures FROZEN and WET. A FROZEN combatant
                          may use a FIRE move; successfully firing it also cures the
                          user.
    WATER                 A connected hit always cures BURNED and applies WET for
                          one round.
    Elemental reactions occur on connected hits, including hits fully negated by
    BLOCK, but not on misses, dodges, or parries. Elemental tags do not affect the
    Power formula, move category, or Black Flash eligibility. They can gate a
    BLOCK/PARRY through blockElementalTags and can alter a block through an
    INCOMING_HIT_HAS_TAG condition.

  IMMEDIATE EFFECTS:
    STUN_CURRENT_ACTION   Cancels the target's not-yet-fired action segment(s)
                          active on the current tick. Authored as a shared move
                          effect row with trigger, target, optional condition,
                          and optional activation chance. HEAVY segments resist.

  OTHER HIT / MOVE MODIFIERS:
    GUARD_BREAK           A hit ignores the defender's BLOCK defensive
                          moves — the block applies no reduction and cannot negate
                          the hit. Dodges and parries are unaffected; only blocks
                          bypass. A guard-break attack is still negated by a parry
                           but does NOT get staggered by one. The old move-wide
                           boolean is migrated to hit tags; Move.isGuardBreak()
                           means at least one hit has it.
    HEAVY                 A move-level tag. An action segment carrying it is IMMUNE to
                          STUN_CURRENT_ACTION. HEAVY does not resist CE depletion,
                          ability cancellation, or the STAGGER status.
                          The "heavy beats stun" half of the stun ↔ heavy
                          interaction. Backed by Move.isHeavy().

  Black Flash eligibility rule (evaluated independently per hit): a component must
    contain both PHYSICAL and CURSED_ENERGY, and must not contain
    INNATE_TECHNIQUE or NON_INNATE_TECHNIQUE.

================================================================================
  6. MOVE & HIT CATEGORIES  (MoveCategory.java, HitComponent.java)
================================================================================

For a damaging action, each hit derives a category from its own exact type-tag set.
That category selects Power and Black Flash eligibility. The parent move keeps a
derived category for UI and compatibility, while pool/slot budget is derived
separately from the parent raw tags (§7). UTILITY and DEFENSIVE are parent action
categories rather than damaging hit categories.

  Category                                Tags                              BF?
  PHYSICAL                                {PHYSICAL}                        No
  CURSED_ENERGY                           {CURSED_ENERGY}                   No   (raw CE attack)
  INNATE_TECHNIQUE                        {INNATE_TECHNIQUE}                No
  NON_INNATE_TECHNIQUE                    {NON_INNATE_TECHNIQUE}            No
  PHYSICAL_CURSED_ENERGY                  {PHYSICAL, CURSED_ENERGY}         Yes
  PHYSICAL_INNATE_TECHNIQUE               {PHYSICAL, INNATE_TECHNIQUE}      No
  PHYSICAL_NON_INNATE_TECHNIQUE           {PHYSICAL, NON_INNATE_TECHNIQUE}  No
  INNATE_NON_INNATE_TECHNIQUE             {INNATE_TECHNIQUE, NON_INNATE_TECHNIQUE}  No
  PHYSICAL_INNATE_NON_INNATE_TECHNIQUE    {all three technique tags}        No
  UTILITY                                 {UTILITY}                         No
  DEFENSIVE                               {DEFENSIVE}                       No

  Black Flash eligible (the only category with PHYSICAL + CURSED_ENERGY and
  no technique tag): PHYSICAL_CURSED_ENERGY.

  MoveData.derivedCategory() collapses DEFENSIVE and UTILITY first (regardless of
  any other tags) and falls back to UTILITY for unknown combinations.

================================================================================
  7. MOVE POOLS & MOVE SLOTS  (MovePool.java, SlotBudgetEnforcer.java)
================================================================================

POOL answers one question: which stat's slot budget does this move draw from?
Pool is orthogonal to category and to tactical function (offensive/defensive/
utility). It is derived from the RAW tag set, not from MoveCategory, because
MoveCategory collapses the PHYSICAL tag away for defensive/utility moves.

  COMBAT_ARTS    Moves carrying the PHYSICAL tag.  Slots ← Combat Ability (raw).
  JUJUTSU_ARTS   Moves WITHOUT the PHYSICAL tag.   Slots ← Jujutsu Skill (raw).

  Hybrid rule: any move containing PHYSICAL counts as a Combat Art, even if it
  also carries technique or CE tags. A non-PHYSICAL move is always a Jujutsu Art
  regardless of whether it is offensive, defensive, or utility.

MOVE SLOTS
  The number of moves of each pool a character can equip. Progression is a
  threshold table (ART_SLOT_TIERS), shared by both pools, read off the RAW
  governing stat — slots are the one derived value that BYPASSES StatScale so
  thresholds land on clean stat milestones instead of distorted numbers.

  stat ≥  :   0   30   45   60   80   100   120   150   180   210   240   270   300
  slots   :   2    3    4    5    6     8     9    10    11    12    13    14    15

  In words: everyone gets 2 of each to start, then +1 at 30/45/60/80, +2 at 100,
  +1 at 120/150, then +1 every 30 points up to a max of 15 at 300. Stats above
  the max tier clamp to 15.
  Governing stats: COMBAT_ABILITY for Combat Arts, JUJUTSU_SKILL for Jujutsu Arts.
  (Cursed Technique Mastery does NOT grant slots.)
  Free moves (isFreeMove) skip only the slot-budget check.

================================================================================
  8. DEFENSE TYPES  (DefenseType.java, BlockStyle.java, DodgeScope.java)
================================================================================

A DEFENSIVE move picks one defense type. Three are implemented (BLOCK, PARRY,
DODGE); SHIELD is reserved.

DEFENSE TIMING AND DURATION
  FIXED      The window opens when the defensive move fires.
  REACTION   Firing arms the defense until round end. The first matching hostile
             attack triggers its window at that attack's impact tick. It triggers
             once per placement and whiffs if no attack arrives.

  Shared blockDuration rules for BLOCK, PARRY, and DODGE:
    blockDuration = 0    use the move's AP Cost as the window length.
    blockDuration = -1   window lasts until end of round.
    blockDuration > 0    window lasts that many ticks.
  A FIXED window is anchored to its fire tick; a REACTION window is anchored to
  the attack that triggers it.

DEFENSE USES
  defenseUses limits how many attacks a live defense may contest; 0 is unlimited.
  A potency-valid BLOCK spends a use when it contests. A successful PARRY spends
  one. DODGE spends one whenever it takes its chance roll, even when that roll
  fails. Spending all uses disables the defense without changing its time window.

DEFENSE COVERAGE PROFILE — BLOCK and PARRY
  Coverage is checked independently for every HitComponent. Three dimensions must
  all accept the incoming hit; an empty dimension is unrestricted.

    blockAttackTypes      Exact one-of-three defensive category membership:
      PHYSICAL                  pure physical
      PHYSICAL_CURSED_ENERGY    physical plus CE or either technique type
      CURSED_ENERGY             non-physical raw CE, innate technique,
                                non-innate technique, or mixed techniques
    blockRanges           Every MELEE/RANGED tag on the hit must be selected. A
                          nonempty range set rejects a hit with no range tag.
    blockElementalTags    Every ICE/ELECTRIC/FIRE/WATER tag on the hit must be
                          selected. A non-elemental hit remains accepted.

  Technique simplification exists only for defensive coverage. It does not change
  the hit's actual tags, Power category, prerequisites, or Black Flash rules.

POTENCY GATE — attack vs defence
  Every attack and every BLOCK/PARRY move carries a potency (1–5). A BLOCK or
  PARRY applies iff its potency ≥ the incoming attack's potency; a higher-potency
  attack blows through a lower-potency defence (the defence is simply ignored).
  DODGE is chance-based and ignores potency. The gate is checked in
  DamageCalculator; the window itself is tracked by Timeline.activeDefenseAt.

GUARD_BREAK interaction
  GUARD_BREAK bypasses BLOCK only. PARRY and DODGE are unaffected: a guard-break
  attack is still parried (negated) and still rolled against by a dodge. A parry
  of a guard-break attack deals no damage but does NOT stagger the attacker.

INTANGIBLE interaction
  INTANGIBLE bypasses both BLOCK and PARRY regardless of potency. Blocking applies
  no reduction and cannot negate the hit; parry outcomes and on-parry effects do
  not fire. DODGE remains unaffected.

NONE
  Not a defensive move. No defence applied.

BLOCK
  Damage reduction. The reduction formula is set by BlockStyle:
    PERCENTAGE  effectiveReduction = min(100,
                  blockDamageReduction × effectiveness multipliers)
                attack value × (1 − effectiveReduction/100).
    FLAT        subtract blockFlatReduction × effectiveness multipliers
                (damage floor 1).
  Applied BEFORE Defense in the damage pipeline. Do NOT call "blockDamageReduction"
  the "Defense" value — Defense is the computed stat applied AFTER the block.
  BLOCK_EFFECTIVENESS_MULTIPLY rows run at BLOCK_CALCULATION against the current
  incoming hit; every matching row's value multiplies together. The row may use a
  condition, activation chance, and mastery progression. Authored 100% can become
  partial, and authored less than 100% can become a full MOVE_BLOCKED outcome.

  INCOMING_HIT_HAS_TAG normally checks the current hit's selected tag. Its
  CURSED_ENERGY option also matches innate/non-innate techniques and physical-plus-
  cursed/technique hits, so it is broader than BlockAttackType.CURSED_ENERGY.
  On a block, onBlockEffects fire on the defender.

PARRY
  A defense that negates a covered hit entirely (outcome PARRIED, no damage roll).
  Parries use the same attack-type, range, and elemental profile as blocks; only a
  profile with all three dimensions empty is unrestricted. Some parries require a
  weapon because that authored move carries a weapon tag, but weapon use is not a
  universal PARRY rule.
  On a successful parry of a non-GUARD_BREAK, non-RANGED hit, the attacker is
  STAGGERED for parryStaggerTicks AP ticks (0 = no stagger). Guard-break and ranged
  hits are still negated but do not stagger their user.
  On a parry, onParryEffects fire on the defender.

DODGE
  While active, each incoming attack matching the dodgeScope has a dodgeChance%
  probability to be avoided entirely (outcome DODGED, no damage). Type-agnostic
  (works vs physical and cursed energy) and NOT potency-gated. dodgeScope
  (DodgeScope): MELEE / RANGED / BOTH.
  On a dodge, onDodgeEffects fire on the defender.

PERFECT READ
  A FIXED defense makes a Perfect Read when its fire tick exactly equals the
  current hit's impact tick. REACTION defenses never qualify.
    BLOCK    fully negates the hit regardless of style or authored reduction.
    DODGE    succeeds automatically.
    PARRY    adds 2 stagger ticks against a melee hit. Against a RANGED hit it
             reflects that hit instead of staggering.
  Reflection reruns the original attacker's Power/outgoing modifiers against that
  attacker's current Defense/incoming modifiers. It contests no defense and cannot
  Black Flash. An off-tick ranged parry only negates the hit.

SHIELD
  Reserved for a future defence flavour. Not yet implemented — the editor exposes
  it as a placeholder and the engine treats it as NONE.

DEFENSE DIALOGUE
  Each type has its own activation/expiry and outcome lines:
    BLOCK activation: "X raises their block! (…)"   expiry: "X drops their guard!"
    PARRY activation: "X readies a parry!"           expiry: "X lowers their parry stance."
    DODGE activation: "X stands ready to dodge!"     expiry: "X drops their ready stance."
  Outcomes (distinct events): MOVE_BLOCKED / MOVE_BLOCK_REDUCED, MOVE_PARRIED,
  MOVE_DODGED — note DODGED ("X dodged Y!") is separate from MOVE_MISSED
  ("Y missed X!").

================================================================================
  9. AP TIMELINE  (Timeline.java, ActionSegment.java, BattlePlan.java)
================================================================================

AP BAR
  The total action points a combatant has per round (Max AP, §3). Filled with
  action segments during planning.

AP TIMELINE
  The ordered sequence of action segments queued for a round, packed from tick 1.
  Empty space between segments is valid (idle ticks). Two boards exist per plan:
  an offensive board and a defensive board; they are merged for legacy resolution.

TICK (a.k.a. AP tick / game tick)
  One unit of the timeline. The action counter advances tick by tick during
  resolution. DEFAULT_GRID_LENGTH = 150; ticks are 1-indexed (1..gridLength).

ACTION COUNTER
  The sweeping counter that advances tick by tick during RESOLUTION. When it
  reaches a segment's startTick: CE is drained. When it reaches its fireTick: the
  move fires.

ACTION SEGMENT
  One move's occupancy on the timeline.
    startTick      ON_START effects run, then CE is drained here.
    fireTick       = startTick + unleashPoint − 1. The move's effect resolves here.
    endTick        = startTick + apCost − 1. The last tick of the segment.
    actualCeCost   CE after efficiency scaling (drained at startTick).
    stunned / fired flags.
  Can be stunned (skipped) by: STUN_CURRENT_ACTION, CE depletion, or an ability
  cancellation. markFired() is final — a fired segment cannot be retro-cancelled
  (a fired block keeps protecting for its AP window).

INSTANT MOVE
  A move with unleashPoint == 1 (fireTick == startTick). Highest priority in
  tie-breaking between characters — fires before other same-tick moves; further
  ties broken by Speed. Within a single character's own moves firing on the
  same tick, defense fires before offense regardless of unleash point.
  The speed ordering applies to defenses too: a defense only contests an
  attack that lands on its tick if it has ALREADY fired (i.e. it won the
  same-tick speed ordering). A slower same-tick defense has not fired yet when
  a faster attack resolves, so it does NOT contest — defending means committing
  the block to fire BEFORE the attack lands, not merely on the same tick.

STUNNED (segment state)
  An action segment removed from firing — by STUN_CURRENT_ACTION, by CE depletion,
  or by an ability cancellation (CANCEL_NEXT_MOVE). Its AP slot is still spent
  and any CE already drained remains spent; the move simply does not fire.
  Log line: "<combatant> was stunned and could not move."

================================================================================
  10. COMBAT PHASES  (BattleState.java)
================================================================================

  Phase enum: PLANNING, RESOLUTION, ROUND_END, BATTLE_OVER.
  Cycle: PLANNING → RESOLUTION → ROUND_END → PLANNING … → BATTLE_OVER.

PLANNING
  Both combatants submit their move queues (AP timelines) for the round. The
  player selects manually; the AI (AIStrategy) selects automatically. Automatic
  statuses timed ROUND_START refresh here.

RESOLUTION
  currentTick starts at 1 and advances tick by tick. Moves fire, damage is
  resolved, stun effects and coded effects are applied.

ROUND END (round maintenance)
  Status effects tick, Black Flash State expiry is checked (tickBfsExpiry),
  defense buffs and block states are cleared, roundNumber increments,
  currentTick resets to 0, ROUND_START automatic statuses re-apply.

BATTLE OVER
  A side has no living actual fighters. Living summons do not prevent its defeat.
  The surviving side wins; simultaneous elimination is a draw (winner is null).

ROUND
  One full cycle of PLANNING → RESOLUTION → ROUND END. roundNumber starts at 1.
  Automatic statuses also fire at FIGHT_START (construction) then each ROUND_START.

================================================================================
  11. DAMAGE CALCULATION  (DamageCalculator.java, PowerCalculator.java)
================================================================================

Pipeline:
  Each HitComponent follows this pipeline on its own impact tick:
  1. DODGE           — a matching active dodge may avoid the hit; Perfect Read
                        makes a fixed dodge automatic.
  2. HIT ROLL        — does the move connect? (neverMiss / guaranteed hit /
                       guaranteed dodge / hitChance roll)
  3. CODED CONNECT   — onAttackConnected runs on a confirmed connection only.
  4. PARRY           — a profile-matching, sufficiently potent parry negates the
                       hit; skipped for INTANGIBLE. Perfect ranged parries reflect.
  5. BLOCK BYPASS    — INTANGIBLE bypasses forced and active blocks; GUARD_BREAK
                       bypasses blocks but not parries.
  6. POWER           — PowerCalculator.compute(category, attackerStats), then run
                       through attacker.modifyBattleStat(POWER).
  7. BLOCK REDUCTION — a profile-matching active BLOCK spends a use, evaluates
                       BLOCK_CALCULATION multipliers, and reduces attackValue.
                       A Perfect Read fully negates before this calculation.
  8. DEFENSE         — max(1, defender.computeCurrentDefense(tick) × codedModifiers.defenseMultiplier()).
  9. DAMAGE FORMULA  — scale, roll, and damage multipliers applied (see below).
  10. BLACK FLASH     — BF roll on an eligible hit; ×2.5 if proc'd.

  DAMAGE FORMULA (DamageCalculator; component means the current hit):
    power       = PowerCalculator.compute(component.category, attackerStats)
    if category == PHYSICAL: power ×= PHYSICAL_POWER_MULTIPLIER  (0.85)
    power       = attacker.modifyBattleStat(POWER, power)
    attackValue = component.basePower × executionBasePowerMultiplier
                  × basePowerMultiplierFor(move) × power
    attackValueAfterBlock = block applied with conditional effectiveness
    defense     = max(1, defender.computeCurrentDefense(tick) × codedModifiers.defenseMultiplier())
    randomRoll  = ROLL_MIN + (1 − ROLL_MIN) × rng.nextDouble()     // ROLL_MIN = 0.85
    rawDamage   = round( (attackValueAfterBlock / defense)
                          × DAMAGE_SCALE × randomRoll
                          × attacker.damageMultiplierFor(move)
                          × defender.incomingDamageMultiplierFor(move)
                          × elementalStatusMultiplier )
    elementalStatusMultiplier = ×2 for ELECTRIC into WET; ×0.5 for MELEE while BURNED
    rawDamage   = max(1, rawDamage)                                 // floor 1 on a hit
    rawDamage   = attacker.modifyBattleStat(DAMAGE_DEALT, rawDamage)
    finalDamage = rawDamage  (× BF_DAMAGE_MULTIPLIER if Black Flash proc'd)

    NOTE: power and defense are computed from StatScale-scaled stats (see STAT
    SCALING above). PHYSICAL Power is dampened by PHYSICAL_POWER_MULTIPLIER so a
    physical Power edge cannot two-hit a peer; CE/technique moves are unaffected.

  DAMAGE_SCALE = 0.42  (private, tuned for longer fights; was 0.5).
  DAMAGE ROLL range = [0.85, 1.0] (±15%).

POWER (PowerCalculator — integer division; cursedEnergyBase is shared;
       every stat input is StatScale-scaled, S(stat)):
  PHYSICAL                            (S(Strength) × 4 + S(CombatAbility)) / 5
  CURSED_ENERGY (base)                (S(CE_Output) × 3 + S(CE_Reserves) × 2 + S(CE_Efficiency)) / 6
  INNATE_TECHNIQUE                    (cursedEnergyBase + S(CursedTechniqueMastery)) / 2
  NON_INNATE_TECHNIQUE                (cursedEnergyBase + S(JujutsuSkill)) / 2
  PHYSICAL_CURSED_ENERGY              (cursedEnergyBase × 3 + physical) / 4
  PHYSICAL_INNATE_TECHNIQUE           (innateTechnique × 4 + physical) / 5
  PHYSICAL_NON_INNATE_TECHNIQUE       (nonInnateTechnique × 3 + physical) / 4
  INNATE_NON_INNATE_TECHNIQUE         (innateTechnique × 3 + nonInnateTechnique × 2) / 5
  PHYSICAL_INNATE_NON_INNATE_TECHNIQUE(physical + innateTechnique × 3 + nonInnateTechnique × 2) / 6
  UTILITY, DEFENSIVE                  0

  OUTCOMES (DamageResult.Outcome):
  MISS      Hit roll failed (or guaranteed dodge). No damage.
  DODGED    A live DODGE defence avoided the attack (dodgeChance roll). No damage.
  PARRIED   A live PARRY negated the hit (coverage- and potency-gated). A melee
             attacker may be STAGGERED; a perfect ranged parry may reflect damage.
  BLOCKED   Connected but fully negated because effective percentage reached 100%,
             a Perfect Read escalated the block, or another full-block rule applied.
  HIT       Connected. Final damage applied (may include partial block reduction).

RESOLUTION ORDER (DamageCalculator.resolve):
  dodge roll (DODGE, chance-based) → hit roll → coded onAttackConnected →
  parry profile/potency check → forceFullBlock → block profile/potency query
  (bypassed by GUARD_BREAK or INTANGIBLE) → Power → Perfect Read or conditional
  block reduction → Defense → damage → Black Flash.

RAW DAMAGE     Damage before the Black Flash multiplier.
FINAL DAMAGE   Damage after all modifiers (block reduction, BF multiplier). What is
               subtracted from the defender's HP.

================================================================================
  12. BLACK FLASH  (CombatStats.java, BattleCombatant.java, DamageCalculator.java)
================================================================================

BLACK FLASH (BF)
  A rare critical hit rolled independently on eligible hit components. A hit must
  contain PHYSICAL + CURSED_ENERGY and no technique tag (§6).
  BF_DAMAGE_MULTIPLIER = ×2.5, applied to raw damage.

BLACK FLASH CHANCE
  Base BF_BASE_CHANCE = 3% (0.03). Modified by BF_CHANCE_ADD and by BFS escalation.

BLACK FLASH STATE (BFS)
  A temporary state entered after a Black Flash proc. Each consecutive BF hit
  while in BFS escalates the BF chance for subsequent moves:
    Normal (not in BFS)                 3%   (BF_BASE_CHANCE)
    BFS, 0 consecutive hits             10%  (BFS_BF_CHANCES[0])
    BFS, 1 consecutive hit              20%  (BFS_BF_CHANCES[1])
    BFS, 2 consecutive hits             35%  (BFS_BF_CHANCES[2])
    BFS, 3+ consecutive hits            50%  (cap, BFS_BF_CHANCES[3])

  State machine (BattleCombatant):
    enterBlackFlashState(round)  on first entry sets inBlackFlashState=true and
                                 resets consecutiveBfsHits=0; always sets
                                 bfsExpiresAfterRound = round + 1 (refreshed each proc).
    recordBfsHit()               increments consecutiveBfsHits (called AFTER
                                 enterBlackFlashState on a re-trigger).
    tickBfsExpiry(completedRound) clears BFS when completedRound ≥ bfsExpiresAfterRound.
    getCurrentBfChance()         returns the array entry if in BFS else BF_BASE_CHANCE,
                                 + bfChanceBonus, through modifyBattleStat(BLACK_FLASH_CHANCE),
                                 capped at 1.0.

  On each BF proc: CE restored = round(maxCE × BF_CE_RESTORE_FRACTION) = 5% of max CE.
  BFS expiry is checked at ROUND_END and emits a BFS_EXPIRED event.

================================================================================
  13. CURSED ENERGY  (CharacterStats.java, CombatStats.java)
================================================================================

CURSED ENERGY (CE)
  A combat resource. Spent when a CE-tagged action segment reaches its startTick.
  Fighters regenerate 0.05 CE per resolution tick by default; fractional CE is
  carried between ticks. CE_REGENERATION battle-stat effects can alter this rate.
  Defense holds at a plateau while the pool can supply the CE Output cap; once
  the pool drops below that cap, Defense degrades.

CE POOL (Max Cursed Energy)
  Total CE at fight start. CE Pool = round(S(CursedEnergyReserves) × CE_POOL_SCALE) (= ×8, scaled).

CE COST (of a move)
  The CE drained when a CE-tagged segment reaches its startTick. Scaled from
  baseCeCost by CE Efficiency and CE Output (see CE COST SCALING), clamped to
  [minCeCost, maxCeCost]. Abilities can force minimum (CE_COST_TO_MINIMUM) or
  multiply (CE_COST_MULTIPLY). Ordered CE_COST_ALTER rows then scale the bounded
  cost and add or subtract a flat amount; each row's result is floored at 0 and
  may intentionally pass the move's configured bounds.

CE COST SCALING
  Efficiency and Output use the SCALED stat values (StatScale.scale, 10→~472);
  raw 10–300 values are scaled once inside CeEfficiencyCalculator.

  Efficiency (scaled), baseline 80 = 1.0×, REDUCES cost (piecewise linear):
    eff ≤ 80:  effMult = (115 - eff) / 35          // 10 = 3.0×, 80 = 1.0×
    eff > 80:  effMult = 1 - 0.0025·(eff - 80)      // 472 = 0.020×

  Output (scaled), ≤80 = 1.0× (flat), RAISES cost above the baseline (linear):
    out ≤ 80:  outMult = 1.0                          // 10 = 1.0×, 80 = 1.0×
    out > 80:  outMult = 1 + (out - 80) / 196          // 472 = 3.0×

  actualCost = baseCeCost × effMult × outMult, clamped to [minCeCost, maxCeCost].
  Cost-neutral reference: Efficiency 80 + Output ≤80 = 1.0× (pays baseCeCost).
  Output only raises cost above the baseline (raw > 80). Efficiency's swing is
  far larger than Output's. The two are MULTIPLICATIVE (not additive):
  eff 472 + out 472 = 0.020 × 3.000 = 0.060×.

CURSED SPIRIT PHYSIOLOGY
  Every CharacterType.CURSED_SPIRIT automatically receives abilities 000051 and
  000052 when its ability source is resolved:
    - Max CE is multiplied by 5. Baseline reserves 80 therefore produce 3,200 CE.
    - CE regeneration is raised from 0.05 to 1.0 CE per resolution tick.
    - A move's final effective CE cost is waived when its authored baseCeCost is
      <= max(1, floor(raw ten-stat BST / 100)). The check uses the authored base
      cost and raw stats, not the scaled cost or ability-modified stats.
    - Reconstitute (move 000131) is granted as a slot-free move usable twice per
      round; each use heals 15% Max HP.

EXORCISE
  Defeat a cursed spirit by reducing it to 0 HP with cursed power. A fatal hit
  containing CURSED_ENERGY, INNATE_TECHNIQUE, or NON_INNATE_TECHNIQUE can
  exorcise. Attacks made by a cursed spirit, cursed corpse, shikigami, or a
  matching equipped cursed tool can also exorcise, as can non-hit direct damage.
  A fatal uncursed PHYSICAL hit from an ordinary attacker instead leaves the
  cursed spirit at 1 HP; this protection can trigger again on later hits.

================================================================================
  14. ABILITIES  (AbilityEffectType.java, AbilityApplicator.java)
================================================================================

ABILITY
  A passive (or, in data, ACTIVE) power attached to a character. Each ability has
  a category (PASSIVE / ACTIVE), a sourceType (CHARACTER / TECHNIQUE / MOVE /
  STAT_THRESHOLD / ABILITY / SHIKIGAMI / CURSED_SPIRIT / CURSED_CORPSE /
  CURSED_TOOL), an optional innate-technique mastery threshold, and a list of
  effects. Mechanics that require compiled code are represented by an editable
  CODED effect row inside that list (see §15); the ability remains an ordinary
  mutable data wrapper and may combine coded and generic effects.

AUTOMATICALLY ASSIGNED ABILITY
  An ability with automaticallyAssigned=true is assigned whenever its source is
  available. This is resolved to a fixed point alongside granted abilities, so
  mandatory class physiology does not need to be stored in each character's
  abilityIds list. Abilities without the flag remain optional.

PASSIVE ABILITY
  Applied once at BattleCombatant construction via AbilityApplicator for
  always-active effects, which persist for the entire fight. Triggered runtime
  effects (HEAL_HP and below) are instead executed by the passive dispatcher when
  the ability's activation predicate fires.

ACTIVE ABILITY
  Linked to a move (activeMoveId) and normalized to subtype QUEUED on save; its
  effects list is cleared. (Combat hook-up for queued actives is incomplete.)

AVAILABLE
  Content shown in the Character Editor's AVAILABLE list. Available content is
  not learned or active until moved into LEARNED (moves) or ASSIGNED (abilities).

GRANT_MOVE        Adds a move to AVAILABLE, bypassing all requirements (it still
                  occupies a slot once learned).
UNLOCK_MOVE       Adds a move to AVAILABLE; it must still be learned normally
                  (prerequisites, technique, slot budget all apply).
GRANT_ABILITY     Adds an ability to AVAILABLE for normal assignment.
UNLOCK_TECHNIQUE  Lets a character learn and use moves belonging to a technique
                  it does not innately possess.

CHARACTER-SOURCE ABILITY
  An ability whose sourceType is CHARACTER. It is available to every character;
  CHARACTER does not mean character-exclusive. Only assigned abilities are active.

ABILITY EFFECT TYPES
  Every type owns its editor label, description, required parameters, defaults,
  and whether it may appear on a move or Domain. Triggered runtime members are in
  the explicit ACTIVATION_REQUIRED set. Move rows add a MoveEffectTrigger (§4);
  Domain rows add Domain trigger/audience/delivery metadata (§20).

  Construction-time stats and markers:
    STAT_ADD / STAT_MULTIPLY / STAT_DIVIDE / STAT_SET_VALUE / STAT_SET_MIN
    STAT_ALLOCATION_MINIMUM / STAT_ALLOCATION_MAXIMUM / STAT_BONUS_POINTS
    POISON_IMMUNITY / SOUL_AWARE_ATTACKS

  Compiled mechanics:
    CODED               Enable an allow-listed compiled ability feature.
    CODED_MOVE_ACTION   Dispatch a move row to a CodedAbilityRuntime (§15).

  Move-scoped CE and accuracy:
    CE_COST_TO_MINIMUM / CE_COST_MULTIPLY / CE_COST_ALTER
    CE_COST_WAIVE_BY_STAT_TOTAL
    MOVE_ACCURACY_ADD / MOVE_ACCURACY_MULTIPLY
    OPPONENT_ACCURACY_ADD / OPPONENT_ACCURACY_MULTIPLY
    NEVER_MISS / NEVER_HIT  Competing accuracy tiers; Never Miss wins a tie.

  Move-scoped damage and block calculation:
    DAMAGE_MULTIPLY              Multiply final outgoing damage.
    MOVE_BASE_POWER_MULTIPLY     Multiply configured Base Power before Power.
    MOVE_BASE_POWER_SCALE_BY_STAT  Scale Base Power on a piecewise stat curve.
    INCOMING_DAMAGE_MULTIPLY     Multiply matching incoming damage.
    BLOCK_EFFECTIVENESS_MULTIPLY Multiply this BLOCK's percentage or flat amount
                                 against the current hit; BLOCK_CALCULATION only.

  Grants and persistent combat rules:
    GRANT_MOVE / UNLOCK_MOVE / GRANT_ABILITY / UNLOCK_TECHNIQUE
    BF_CHANCE_ADD / MODIFY_DEFENSE / DEFENSE_FROM_DURABILITY / MODIFY_AP_BAR
    SET_JUJUTSU_ART_SLOTS / AUTO_STATUS_APPLY / LOCK_MOVE_TAG
    COST_CE_PER_ROUND / MAX_ACTIVE_SUMMONS / SUMMON_CE_UPKEEP_PER_ACTIVE_TICK
    BATTLE_STAT_ODDS_MULTIPLY   Multiply odds without directly multiplying chance.
    BATTLE_STAT_MODIFIER       Permanently add, multiply, or SET a battle stat.

  Triggered HP, CE, damage, and status:
    HEAL_HP / RESTORE_CE       Flat or percent-of-maximum, selected by VALUE_MODE.
    DRAIN_CE                   Flat or percent; immediate or over time.
    DEAL_DIRECT_DAMAGE         Flat or max-HP percent; bypasses accuracy/Defense.
    INSTANT_KILL               Reduce to 0 HP unless fatal protection applies.
    APPLY_STATUS / REMOVE_STATUS / CLEAR_STATUSES

  Triggered timed stats:
    TIMED_STAT_MODIFIER        Add, multiply, or set a core/battle stat for a
                               duration, using flat or percentage VALUE_MODE.
    TEMP_STAT_SET_VALUE        Timed exact base-stat value.
  Percentage additions stack additively: +20% then +20% = +40%. For a battle
  stat, the most recently applied live SET wins and pins the exact value,
  overriding additions, multipliers, and percentages until it expires.

  Triggered defenses, accuracy, locks, and targeting:
    IGNORE_DAMAGE / DAMAGE_SHIELD / SURVIVE_FATAL_DAMAGE
    APPLY_NEVER_MISS / APPLY_NEVER_HIT / GUARANTEE_NEXT_BLACK_FLASH
    CANCEL_NEXT_MOVE / STUN_CURRENT_ACTION
    TEMP_LOCK_MOVE_TAG / TEMP_LOCK_TECHNIQUE
    TAUNT / EXCHANGE_ATTACK_TARGETS

  Bounded resources:
    DEFINE_BOUNDED_RESOURCE   Create a named, player-visible value with a starting
                              amount and capacity.
    TRANSACT_BOUNDED_RESOURCE Atomically spend and/or gain named resources.
    CONSUME_BOUNDED_RESOURCE_FOR_BASE_POWER
                              At ON_START consume all of one resource and multiply
                              configured Base Power by the amount consumed.

  Summons, forms, and Domains:
    MOVE_UNAVAILABLE_WHILE_OWNED_SUMMON_ACTIVE  Prevent use while the selected
                                                owned summon remains active.
    SUMMON_CHARACTER / DESUMMON_OWNED_SHIKIGAMI / DESUMMON_TARGET_SHIKIGAMI
    TRANSFORM_CHARACTER      Swap to an authored form while preserving battle identity.
    ESTABLISH_DOMAIN         Establish the selected Domain definition (§20).

  Effect target (AbilityEffectTarget): SELF, ENEMY, BOTH.
  Automatic timing (AbilityEffectTiming): FIGHT_START, ROUND_START, ON_HIT.
  A ROUND_START AUTO_STATUS_APPLY must last exactly 1 round / 0 ticks so it
  refreshes without stacking. Move-scoped rows match a blank scope or
  move.hasTag(moveTag); hit-only tags are available through Move's aggregate query.

================================================================================
  15. CODED ABILITIES  (coded/*.java, CodedAbilityRegistry.java)
================================================================================

Some mechanics cannot be composed from generic effect types. These are
implemented as COMPILED CODED ABILITIES and exposed in data through effect rows:
an ability uses an AbilityEffectData row with type CODED, codedAbilityKey, and
codedFeature; a move uses a self/on-hit effect carrying codedAbilityKey and
codedAction. Moves and abilities stay editable data wrappers, while the hardcoded
behaviour lives in a CodedAbilityRuntime selected by the effect row.

The registry (CodedAbilityRegistry) is an allow-list. At combatant construction
it collects every coded ability key the character has (from CODED rows on assigned
abilities and from coded effect rows on known moves), validates each binding, and
instantiates one runtime per key. Unknown bindings log a warning and are skipped.

CodedAbilityRuntime hooks (all optional except onTrigger, drainPendingEvents, state):
  onTrigger(state, trigger)            activation predicate fired
  onEffectFired(state, effect, attacker, defender, tick)  coded effect row fired
  onAttackConnected(attacker, defender, move, tick[, component], rng)
                                       → CodedHitModifiers (pre-block/defense);
                                         component-aware overloads exist
  beforeIncomingMove(state, attacker, defender, move, tick) → CodedMoveResponse
  tickTimelineEffects(tick)            advance universal-tick timers
  getRemainingTimelineEffectTicks()    longest remaining AP-tick timer owned (view)
  preventFatalDamage()                 true if a fatal hit should be negated this tick
  onOwnedSummonDestroyed(state, owner, destroyedSummon)  record permanent rest-of-
                                       battle state when an owned summon is defeated
  drainPendingEvents(tick)             flush queued events
  state()                              → CodedAbilityState snapshot for the view

================================================================================
  16. STATUS EFFECTS  (StatusEffectType.java)
================================================================================

Temporary effects applied through unified move/Domain rows or ability effects such
as AUTO_STATUS_APPLY, APPLY_STATUS, and TIMED_STAT_MODIFIER. Legacy attachment
lists migrate into those rows. Stat effects
have a non-negative magnitude and an explicit direction in the type name
(*_INCREASE adds, *_DECREASE subtracts).

Duration rules (StatusEffect.validateDuration):
  rounds > 0, ticks = 0      round-only
  rounds = 0, ticks > 0      tick-only
  both > 0                   full rounds, then that many ticks of the next round
  rounds = -1, ticks = 0     permanent

Every base stat has both directions (StatKey-backed):
  VITALITY, STRENGTH, DURABILITY, SPEED, COMBAT_ABILITY,
  CURSED_ENERGY_RESERVES, CURSED_ENERGY_EFFICIENCY,
  CURSED_ENERGY_OUTPUT, JUJUTSU_SKILL, CURSED_TECHNIQUE_MASTERY
    → *_INCREASE / *_DECREASE

Every runtime battle stat has both directions (BattleStatKey-backed):
  MAX_HP, MAX_CURSED_ENERGY (MAX_CE), MAX_AP, ACCURACY, EVASION, POWER, DEFENSE
    → *_INCREASE / *_DECREASE

Non-stat control statuses:
  STAGGER
    Must use rounds = 0 and ticks > 0; its magnitude is unused (stored as 0).
    On the tick it is applied and on every remaining active AP tick, it stuns
    the affected character's active, not-yet-fired action segments. This is a
    character status, not STUN_CURRENT_ACTION, so HEAVY does not resist it.

  SLEEP
    Must use rounds > 0 with 0 ticks. When an affected combatant reaches an
    action's fire tick, that action fails and the battle log names the attempted
    move. Damage wakes the combatant immediately and allows later actions. It
    also has a 5% chance to end at the start of each resolution tick; otherwise
    SLEEP expires at the end of the current round.

  RESTRAINED
    Multiplies Speed by 0.5. Each active tick rolls both Strength-based escape and
    a separate chance to stun the current action.

  WET
    Multiplies Speed by 0.8. ELECTRIC damage against the target is doubled, and an
    ICE hit's FROZEN chance is raised from 5% to 50%. Connected WATER hits apply
    it for one round, while a connected FIRE hit cures it.

  FROZEN
    Multiplies Defense by 0.5 and prevents non-FIRE actions. It has a 10% chance
    to end each active tick. A connected FIRE hit cures it; a FROZEN combatant can
    also fire a FIRE move, curing itself when the move successfully fires.

  BURNED
    Halves outgoing MELEE damage and deals exactly 0.03% max-HP damage per active
    tick, preserving fractional damage between ticks. A FIRE hit has a 10% chance
    to apply it for one round, while any connected ICE or WATER hit cures it.

  FATIGUED
    Adds two AP ticks to both the AP cost and firing point of each planned move.

  CURSED_SPEECH_WARD
    Covers the holder's ears in cursed energy. Incoming Cursed Speech commands
    automatically fail while it is active. An authored ward may also carry CE
    upkeep drained each resolution tick. Reapplication refreshes rather than stacks.

  CURSED_ENERGY_PARASITE
    When the holder actually pays CE for a move, after the drain and before the
    move fires, the most recent applier deals max-HP damage based on the amount:
      1–19 CE → 2%     20–39 CE → 4%     40+ CE → 6%
    Damage is rounded with a minimum request of 1 and wakes SLEEP. A zero-cost or
    fully waived move does not trigger it. Reapplication refreshes one instance
    and replaces its recorded source; the applying effect owns the duration.

  POISON
    Multiplies all ten base character stats by 0.8 and deals exactly 0.06% max-HP
    damage per active tick, preserving fractional damage between ticks. Must use
    rounds > 0 with 0 ticks, or be permanent. Potency is fixed; magnitude is unused.
    Reapplication refreshes its duration instead of stacking. POISON_IMMUNITY
    prevents application.

Total: 17 stat pairs plus 10 specialized statuses = 44 constants.

Legacy aliases (StatusEffectType.fromName resolves these; "signed" names can mean
either direction depending on the stored magnitude's sign):
  FOCUS → ACCURACY_INCREASE
  CE_OUTPUT_UP → CURSED_ENERGY_OUTPUT_INCREASE
  SPEED_UP → SPEED_INCREASE
  POWER_UP → POWER_INCREASE
  DEFENSE_UP → DEFENSE_INCREASE
  BIND → EVASION_DECREASE
  CURSED_SEAL → CURSED_ENERGY_EFFICIENCY_DECREASE
  AP_DRAIN → MAX_AP_DECREASE
Old fractional Power/Defense/Accuracy magnitudes are multiplied by 100 into flat
stat points (normalizeStoredMagnitude).

  17. COMBAT EVENTS  (CombatEvent.java)
================================================================================

Immutable records emitted by CombatResolver and consumed by the View layer (the
Observer bridge — the core never calls the view directly). These are the
canonical names for what happened on a given tick (tick = 0 for system/round
events). Events carry the relevant source/target/move/value/tick and may also carry
a related target, hit-component index, coded-resource snapshot, form identity, or
Domain instance/definition/collapse metadata.

  Move execution:
    MOVE_STARTED        segment reached startTick; start effects/resources resolve
    MOVE_FIRED          move unleashed — attack/effects resolved
    MOVE_MISSED         hit roll failed
    MOVE_BLOCKED        block fully negated damage after all effectiveness rules
    MOVE_BLOCK_REDUCED  block reduced but did not fully negate damage
    MOVE_DODGED         defender's DODGE defense avoided the attack entirely
    MOVE_PARRIED        PARRY negated the hit (may stagger or reflect)
    MOVE_STUNNED        a stun effect, active STAGGER status, CE depletion, or
                         ability cancellation removed this action segment

  Targeting / defense:
    TARGET_RETARGETED   a single-target move's invalid target was retargeted at fire
    TARGETS_EXCHANGED   a target-exchange window transposed an attack recipient
    DEFENSE_GRANTED     an ally-targeted active-defense window was conferred

  Summoning / forms:
    MOVE_SUMMON         a move effect row enqueued a shikigami summon
                         (pre-materialization)
    CHARACTER_TRANSFORMED / CHARACTER_REVERTED  authored form changed/restored

  Generic effects:
    EFFECT_FAILED       a composed effect was admitted but could not complete
    RESOURCE_CHANGED    a named bounded resource changed

  Domains:
    DOMAIN_DECLARED / DOMAIN_ESTABLISHED / DOMAIN_COUNTER_ESTABLISHED
    DOMAIN_CLASH_STARTED / DOMAIN_CLASH_ENDED / DOMAIN_BARRIER_DAMAGED
    DOMAIN_SURE_HIT_APPLIED / DOMAIN_SURE_HIT_NEGATED / DOMAIN_COLLAPSED

  Damage:
    DAMAGE_DEALT        HP was deducted from a combatant
    DAMAGE_IGNORED      incoming damage was negated (IGNORE_DAMAGE / shield)

    MAX_HP_CHANGED      max HP changed (battle stat effect)

    CE_DRAINED          CE was spent on a move

  Status:
    STATUS_APPLIED      a status effect or block message was applied

  Combatants / summons:
    COMBATANT_SUMMONED  a shikigami was materialized onto a team

    COMBATANT_REMOVED   a combatant left the battle (e.g. desummoned)

  BFS:
    BFS_ENTERED         combatant entered Black Flash State
    BFS_EXPIRED         Black Flash State ended (round maintenance)

  Abilities:
    ABILITY_ACTIVATED   a triggered/coded ability activated
    RATIO_TRIGGERED     a Ratio mark fired on a connected hit

  Round / battle:
    ROUND_START         round-start processing began
    ROUND_END           end of the round (used by the view)
    BATTLE_OVER         fight ended; winner determined

================================================================================
  18. AI  (AIStrategy.java, ArchetypeAIStrategy.java)
================================================================================

AI STRATEGY
  Interface that selects BattlePlans using the shared RandomSource so seeded
  battles remain reproducible. Team planning receives the full active roster for
  targeting, defense, summons, and multi-target decisions.

ARCHETYPE AI STRATEGY  (default dispatcher)
  Routes a fighter to a technique/kit-aware planner when one exists, otherwise to
  the generic planners. Shipped strategies include Greedy, Aggressive Sorcerer,
  Passive Sorcerer, Shikigami, Ten Shadows, Cursed Speech, Ratio, Blood
  Manipulation, and Hanami. SmartAIScoring supplies shared move/target scoring.

  Domain-aware planning removes unavailable Domain declarations, values ordinary
  Domain openings, answers a hostile Domain with anti-Domain options, and lowers
  the value of an opening dominated by enemy counters.

GREEDY AI STRATEGY  (generic fallback)
  A greedy, lightly-randomised round planner — NOT a power sort. A single loop
  keeps committing moves until none is affordable (AP and CE) or placeable.

  Selection — weighted-random pick favouring offence:
    WEIGHT_OFFENSIVE = 6.0,  WEIGHT_DEFENSIVE = 2.5,  WEIGHT_UTILITY = 1.0.
    Candidates are partitioned into offensive (ATTACK tag) / defensive
    (move.isDefensive()) / utility. Defensive moves are gated out until at least
    one offensive segment is placed (unless no offensive move is possible).
    A weighted pool is rolled each iteration. The same move may be committed
    multiple times in a round while budgets allow.

  Affordability filter — known moves that fit both AP and CE budgets
    (plan.fitsBudgets), are not locked by an ability (lockedMoveTags), and are
    not in this round's "stuck" set.

  Placement:
    Offensive / utility moves — grouped early on the offensive board, placed
      right after the last segment (tick 1 on an empty board) with a small
      random 0–2 tick gap.
    Defensive moves — their FIRE tick is aligned to a randomly chosen placed
      offensive segment's fire tick (start = max(1, targetFireTick − unleashPoint + 1)),
      so the block is active when the attack lands. This same-tick alignment only
      contests an attack when the AI is fast enough to fire first (defenses only
      protect if they've already fired); when the AI is strictly slower the
      alignment is skipped and the defense falls back to grouped-early so its
      budget isn't wasted on a tick where it can't contest. Also falls back to
      grouped-early if alignment cannot be placed.

  Termination — because the timeline only accumulates, a move that fails to
    place once can never fit later this round; it is added to a per-round "stuck"
    set and never retried.

HANAMI AI STRATEGY
  Uses Flower Offering and Disaster Plants deliberately: a charged Life-Force
  Beam is fired immediately; at zero charges it tries to place the cheapest
  generator before the beam when both fit. Ordinary attacks are capped at two per
  round. It favors Cursed Bud against CE-bearing targets without a parasite,
  RESTRAINED against faster targets, and Flower Field more heavily against groups.
  Its defense selection uses the highest-value available defense and aligns it to
  the most relevant threat.

================================================================================
  19. EDITORS  (graphics .../screens/editors/*.java)
================================================================================

The six authoring screens are Move, Character, Ability, Technique, Cursed Tool,
and Domain editors. They are LibGDX Scene2D screens with shared CRUD chrome, not
CLI tools. Authoring mode is enabled with -Djjktbf.authoring=true.

Re-sequencing: deleting an entity resequences all ids and remaps references in
characters, abilities, coded targets, and technique trees.

MOVE EDITOR  (MoveEditorScreen)
  Saves data/moves/all_moves.json. Parent move tags and per-component hit tags are
  edited separately. Attack authoring includes ordered hit components, AOE shape,
  and pair targeting. Defense authoring includes FIXED/REACTION timing, use cap,
  attack-type/range/element coverage, ally targeting, and Conditional Block
  Effectiveness rows. Unified effects expose every valid trigger, including
  ON_START and BLOCK_CALCULATION, plus effect types such as ESTABLISH_DOMAIN.
  Validation runs MoveData.toMove(), the same conversion used by the engine.

CHARACTER EDITOR  (CharacterEditorScreen)
  Creates/edits characters; saves data/characters/all_characters.json. Sections:
  identity (name/description/title/sprite/innate-technique select), a Type dropdown
  (Sorcerer / Shikigami / Cursed Spirit / Cursed Corpse), a directly-selectable checkbox, a Manual / Point-Buy
  toggle, ten StatField sliders, a live derived-stat preview (HP, AP bar, Acc,
  Evasion, effective CE pool, CE regeneration, raw-BST CE-waiver threshold,
  Combat Arts / Jujutsu Arts slot counts), a MoveAssignmentPanel
  (slot-gated, technique/prerequisite-filtered, drag-and-drop), an ability
  assignment panel, and a SkillTreeCanvas per accessible technique.
  availableDomainIds stores Domain definitions unlocked directly or by a
  technique-tree DOMAIN node.

  title        Optional free-text display epithet (e.g. "SHIKIGAMI",
               "The Honored One"). Display-only — carries no combat rules. Blank
               = no title shown.
  type         CharacterType: SORCERER (default), SHIKIGAMI, CURSED_SPIRIT, or
               CURSED_CORPSE. Shikigami are not directly selectable unless
               overridden and are the only definitions accepted by summon effects.

  Constants: STAT_MIN=10, STAT_MAX=300, BASELINE=80,
    POINT_BUDGET_WITH_TECHNIQUE     = 1000
    POINT_BUDGET_WITHOUT_TECHNIQUE  = 1080   (+80 when CTM is locked at N/A)
  Point-buy math: budget = base + resolvedAbilities(cd).statBonusPoints() (a
  STAT_BONUS_POINTS ability extends the budget); pointsSpent sums (stat − 80)
  across stats, skipping CTM when the character has no technique.
  STAT_ALLOCATION_MINIMUM effects raise individual slider floors and save-time
  validation without changing battle-time stats or blocking temporary debuffs.

ABILITY EDITOR  (AbilityEditorScreen)
  Creates/edits abilities; saves data/abilities/all_abilities.json. Category
  {PASSIVE, ACTIVE}; sourceType {CHARACTER, TECHNIQUE, MOVE, STAT_THRESHOLD,
  ABILITY, SHIKIGAMI, CURSED_SPIRIT, CURSED_CORPSE, CURSED_TOOL}; an automatic
  assignment checkbox controls mandatory source-provided abilities. Every ability
  uses EffectListEditor; CODED rows select an allow-listed compiled feature and
  remain addable, editable, and removable like generic rows.
  Active abilities with generic effects use ConditionTreeEditor (nested AND/OR)
  and an optional activation chance (0–100%). Validation enforces unique name,
  valid category/source, source-specific references, effect parameters, and the
  passive/active restrictions owned by each effect type.

TECHNIQUE EDITOR  (TechniqueEditorScreen)
  Creates/edits innate techniques and their skill trees; saves
  data/techniques/all_techniques.json. A SkillTreeCanvas authoring surface:
  drag nodes, right-click to attach/edit prerequisites. Save rejects duplicate
  names, rewrites technique-name references in moves (requiredTechniqueId),
  abilities (sourceValue / UNLOCK_TECHNIQUE), characters (innateTechniqueName),
  and ordinary Domains (requiredTechniqueName) on rename, and writes mastery/stat
  prerequisites back onto the technique's content. Delete is blocked while a
  move, ability, or Domain references the technique.

  Skill tree content may be MOVE, ABILITY, or DOMAIN. Prerequisites
  (SkillTreePrerequisiteData): NODE (nodeId, attached), MASTERY (minimum),
  STAT (stat, minimum).

CURSED TOOL EDITOR  (CursedToolEditorScreen)
  Saves data/tools/all_tools.json. A tool provides a WeaponType and may grant
  authored moves or abilities while equipped.

DOMAIN EDITOR  (DomainEditorScreen)
  Saves data/domains/all_domains.json. Authors ordinary or anti-Domain topology,
  membership policies, duration/upkeep, barriers and clash pressure, counter
  behavior, and the five ordered effect channels described in §20. For an ordinary
  Domain, save creates or synchronizes its prerequisite-bearing DOMAIN node in the
  required technique's tree; mastery/stat prerequisites are edited on that node.

================================================================================
  20. DOMAINS  (model/domain/*.java, data/domains/all_domains.json)
================================================================================

DOMAIN
  A battle-wide authored field established by an ESTABLISH_DOMAIN move effect.
  An ORDINARY DOMAIN may belong to a required innate technique and may provide
  sure-hit, field, caster, barrier, and procedure programs. An ordinary field
  with no required technique is non-innate. An ANTI-DOMAIN uses the same data
  model but supplies sure-hit nullification rather than an ordinary sure-hit.

DECLARATION AND OWNERSHIP
  An ordinary Domain requires its definition to be unlocked. If it belongs to
  an innate technique, that technique must be usable and not temporarily locked.
  Anti-Domains bypass those technique checks. A combatant may maintain only one
  field total, whether it is an ordinary Domain or an anti-Domain. Declaring any
  other field while one is active fails and leaves the existing field unchanged.
  A second declaration from the same combatant also fails while the first is
  pending. Declarations from different combatants on one tick establish atomically
  before any ON_ESTABLISH program runs, so same-tick declarations interact fairly.

TOPOLOGY
  Authoring classification of a field's structure. Exterior barriers and the
  OPEN-vs-CLOSED pressure rule were retired with the clash rework, so topology
  no longer changes clash behaviour; open Domains canonically carry a higher
  authored clash value instead.
    CLOSED       A barrier-sealed interior.
    OPEN         Projects into the base battlefield with no outer wall.
    INCOMPLETE   A partial field, used by the shipped Simple Domain anti-Domain.

MEMBERSHIP POLICIES
  Capture (who enters on establishment; the owner is always included):
    EVERYONE / SELECTED_TARGETS
  Entrants (how later summons/combatants join):
    SNAPSHOT / FOLLOW_SUMMONER / ALL_NEW_COMBATANTS
  Protection (members exempt from hostile Domain programs):
    NONE / OWNER / OWNER_AND_ALLIES / OWNER_AND_SELECTED

DURATION, UPKEEP, AND INTEGRITY
  A Domain has round/tick duration, fractional CE upkeep per active tick, and
  internal barrier integrity. Fractional upkeep debt is carried until a whole
  CE point is due; failure to pay collapses the Domain. Internal integrity is
  the field's barrier health: it is damaged or repaired by barrier program rows
  and damaged by Domain clash pressure. Reaching zero collapses the Domain.
  Maintenance also fails immediately when one connected hit removes more than
  8% of the owner's maximum HP, or whenever the owner falls below 15% maximum HP.
  A combatant below 15% HP cannot establish a new Domain or anti-Domain.

DOMAIN PROGRAMS
  Five ordered effect channels reuse the generic AbilityEffectType vocabulary:
    sureHitEffects / fieldEffects / casterEffects / barrierEffects /
    procedureEffects
  Each row has a condition tree, optional activation chance, interval, and:
    Trigger:   ON_ESTABLISH / ON_MEMBER_ENTER / EACH_TICK / ON_ROUND_START /
               ON_ROUND_END / ON_COLLAPSE
    Audience:  OWNER / ENTERING_MEMBER / ENEMY_MEMBERS / ALLY_MEMBERS /
               ALL_MEMBERS / UNPROTECTED_MEMBERS / BARRIER / EXTERIOR_ENEMIES
    Delivery:  EFFECT / ATTACK / INFORMATION / RULE
  Sure-hit rows alone pass through clash suppression, member protection, and
  anti-Domain counter checks before execution. Field rows describe effects of
  the environment, while caster rows describe effects sourced by the owner;
  both otherwise use normal row execution and their authored audience. Barrier
  rows with the BARRIER audience repair this field's integrity or damage hostile
  barriers. Procedure rows use normal execution for authored rules and scripted
  lifecycle behavior; interactive procedures remain deferred. Anti-Domains may
  define barrier and procedure rows, but not sure-hit, field, or caster rows.
  BARRIER rows can repair integrity with HEAL_HP or damage another barrier with
  DEAL_DIRECT_DAMAGE / INSTANT_KILL. Effects leased from a Domain are removed
  when it collapses; ON_COLLAPSE programs run first unless the battle itself ended.

DOMAIN CLASH
  Overlapping hostile ordinary Domains clash. Both sure-hits are suppressed
  while the clash exists. Each side holds a clash score:

    score = clashValue × (1 + (3 × refinement + cePressure) / 100) / 20

    clashValue   The Domain's authored base clash value (similar for standard
                 Domains; open and specialty non-hit Domains author higher).
    refinement   DOMAIN REFINEMENT of the owner: one part Jujutsu Skill and
                 one part Cursed Technique Mastery, read from live effective
                 stats.
    cePressure   The owner's current CE pool capped by CE output, as a
                 percentage of the maximum pool. Re-read every tick, so upkeep
                 drain shifts the clash live.

  Refinement carries exactly three times the weight of the CE factor, so
  refinement decides most clashes. Each tick, the lower-scoring Domain loses
  internal integrity equal to ceil(abs(first score - second score)). Equal
  scores deal no integrity damage. Takeover progress displays the fraction of
  the weaker Domain's maximum integrity already lost. If live CE pressure makes
  leadership flip, subsequent damage and the displayed leader flip with it.
  Reaching zero integrity collapses the loser with INTERNAL_BARRIER_BROKEN;
  the winner remains established and its sure hit resumes.

  The battle screen shows active fields in [DOMAIN] / [ANTI-DOMAIN] strips
  with remaining duration; the leading side of a clash is marked
  <<CLASH nn%>> with its takeover progress.

ANTI-DOMAIN NULLIFICATION
  "Counter type" classifies which sure-hit delivery classes an anti-Domain can
  nullify; it does not launch a retaliatory move. Counter potency must be at
  least the hostile Domain's authored clashValue.
    SURE_HIT_NULLIFICATION          blocks every delivery class.
    SURE_HIT_INTERCEPTION           blocks ATTACK only.
    TECHNIQUE_CONTACT_NULLIFICATION blocks EFFECT, ATTACK, and INFORMATION, but
                                    cannot answer RULE.
  A successful negation consumes one use only when a finite number is authored.
  Reaching zero collapses that anti-Domain; -1 continuously nullifies qualifying
  sure hits for as long as the anti-Domain remains maintained. Anti-Domains do
  not clash normally: each overlapping hostile Domain damages their integrity
  every tick by ceil(the hostile Domain's full live clash score).

COLLAPSE AND TECHNIQUE BURNOUT
  Canonical reasons: DURATION_EXPIRED, OWNER_DEFEATED, OWNER_REMOVED,
  OWNER_DAMAGED, OWNER_LOW_HP,
  UPKEEP_FAILED, CLASH_LOST, INTERNAL_BARRIER_BROKEN, COUNTER_EXHAUSTED,
  OWNER_ACTED, VOLUNTARY, BATTLE_ENDED.
  Unless battle end suppresses cleanup effects, an ordinary innate-technique
  Domain's collapse applies TEMP_LOCK_TECHNIQUE to its required technique for
  the authored burnout duration. Non-innate fields and anti-Domains have no
  innate technique to burn out and do not receive technique burnout.

SHIPPED SIMPLE DOMAIN
  Domain 000000 Simple Domain is an INCOMPLETE anti-Domain. It carves out and
  protects its owner's space, has 3000 base internal integrity, no upkeep, and
  unlimited Potency 100 TECHNIQUE_CONTACT_NULLIFICATION. It continuously stops
  qualifying sure hits while maintained; heavier specialty clash values can
  exceed its potency. Move 000138 only establishes this field. Move 000026 also
  provides a separate Potency-1 New Shadow Style parry that answers a successfully
  parried melee attacker with 000025 Sword Quick Draw. That automatic sword
  response belongs to the move stance, not Simple Domain nullification.
