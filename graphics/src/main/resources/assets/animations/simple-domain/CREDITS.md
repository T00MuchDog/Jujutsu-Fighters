# Simple Domain

Original deterministic pixel geometry authored for Jujutsu Fighters. No anime,
manga, game screenshot, or third-party sprite pixels are included in these sheets.

Source: `scripts/build_simple_domain_animations.py`, revised `quiet-chalk` pair:
blue filled base and circulating pointed wavelets, following the user's supplied
Miwa anime still and two manga panels.
Logical 96x96, exported 192x192 using nearest-neighbour 2x; transparent or opaque
pixels only. Five-color selected palette: dark teal, two floor blues, pale cyan,
and cool near-white. No reference pixels are copied into the generated art.

Visual research, candidate reviews and validation instructions:
`docs/animations/simple-domain/README.md`.

Simple Domain and Jujutsu Kaisen are references to Gege Akutami's work. The anime
visual references are credited to the Jujutsu Kaisen anime production; links are
research references, not licenses for redistribution of reference artwork.
